# Nice to have

Ideas that are not planned yet, kept here so they are not forgotten.

- **Wire channel in the deep base.** A slot from the LED-module pocket out through the
  side of the base (configurable width, height and side), like the reference
  "forhøjet 5.17 mm" base, so a module with wires can be used.
- **Inline emoji in Text mode.** Emoji typed between letters in the Text tab, laid out on
  the text baseline together with the glyphs (Blocks and the Emoji tab already trace emoji).
- **Two-tone body.** The bezel ring and the floor of the base as separate parts so they
  can be printed in different colours.
- **Engraved label on the side of the base.** A short text recessed into the outer wall.
- **Low-profile switches.** Kailh Choc socket and stem assets as an alternative to MX.
